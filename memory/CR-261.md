## Why

Asha (AI voice agent) calls every new lead within 60 seconds of form submission. The workflow that triggers her call fires at **contact creation** in Freshsales. Today she has to ask everything on that first call — business name, billing method, segment, status — because the form doesn't send it.

Two campaign pages are live right now:
- **Homepage** — full form (6 fields)
- **Petpooja comparison page** — `shortForm=true` (3 required + 1 optional)

The Petpooja page loses the most data. `business_name` is optional and blank 43% of the time. `outlet_type`, `years_in_business`, billing method — all hidden. Asha goes in blind.

A second problem: `cf_est_name` and `cf_outlet_type` are both polluted with ad-tracking values and landing-page slugs. Sales and reports can't use them reliably.

## Rules (from brief — non-negotiable)

1. **Tier 1 fields must land in Freshsales at contact creation.** Asha's trigger fires then. Tier 2 fields can update the contact later.
2. **Ad-tracking values go only into tracking CFs.** Never into `job_title`, `cf_outlet_type`, `cf_business_status`.
3. **Use fixed values exactly.** Freshsales dropdowns will reject unlisted values silently.
4. **Provide the Freshsales API name of every new or reused field.** ✅ Done above.
5. **Test on 5 test leads before go-live.** Check every field lands in Freshsales. Send the 5 Freshsales contact IDs back as evidence.
