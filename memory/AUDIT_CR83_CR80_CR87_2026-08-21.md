**🔴 CRITICAL — `/pricing`** (CR-80 — already registered)

- Implementation: Add to `/pricing` AND `/` (homepage) to establish the entity globally

**🟠 HIGH — `/customers`** (CR-89 — already registered)
- 6 real customer testimonials with names + outlet names exist
- Missing: `AggregateRating` + `Review` schema
- Dependency: Owner to supply individual owner names/designations first

**🟡 MEDIUM — Homepage `/`**
- Has `Organization` schema but not `SoftwareApplication`
- Once CR-80 creates `SOFTWARE_APP_JSONLD`, it should be added to homepage too
- Low risk — additive only

**LOW — `/petpooja-alternative`**
- Comparison table and testimonials are schema-eligible (`ComparisonList`, `Review`)
- Not urgent — page is noindex-adjacent (ad landing page)

## PART 3 — CR-87: /demo Landing Page Full Audit

### Current state of `/demo`

**Page purpose (as built):** Cold Meta/Google ad traffic — discovery stage. "I haven't heard of MyGenie, show me what it is."

**Owner's new purpose (from brief):** Competitor comparison traffic — consideration stage. "I'm using GoFrugal/UrbanPiper, is MyGenie better?"

These are fundamentally different audiences. The page needs 4 targeted tweaks, not a rebuild.

---

### Current vs Required — The 4 Tweaks

#### Tweak 1 — H1
| | |
|---|---|
| **Current** | "See MyGenie live — built for your restaurant" |
| **Problem** | "See MyGenie live" = discovery copy. Comparison shopper already knows they want a demo — they're asking "is MyGenie better than what I have?" |
| **Required** | "Looking for a Better Restaurant POS?" OR "Compare MyGenie With Your Current POS" |
| **Where to change** | `DEMO_DEFAULTS.headline` in `DemoLanding.jsx` line 49 |

#### Tweak 2 — Stat reframe
| | Current | Required |
|---|---|---|
| Stat 1 | ₹1L+ leakage caught in 2 weeks | ✅ Keep — powerful switching trigger |
| **Stat 2** | **"48hr from sign-up to first bill"** | **"48hr to switch" — reframe as migration speed** (someone on a competitor cares about switching cost, not setup time) |
| Stat 3 | +18% avg bill via AI upsell | ✅ Keep — unique differentiator |
| **Where to change** | `ProofCard` at line 94: `label="from sign-up to first bill"` → `label="to switch from your current POS"` |

#### Tweak 3 — Add 5th walkthrough bullet
| | |
|---|---|
| **Current** | 4 bullets: Live billing demo · Your leakage report · AI features live · Your pricing |
| **Add** | 5th bullet: **"Side-by-side comparison"** — "We show you exactly how MyGenie stacks up against your current setup, feature by feature." |
| **Why** | Directly addresses the comparison-shopper mindset. No competitor is named. |
| **Where to change** | Array at lines 110–113 in `DemoLanding.jsx` — add 5th item |

#### Tweak 4 — Trust line below CTA button
| | |
|---|---|
| **Current** | "100s of outlets across 75 cities already on MyGenie" |
| **Required** | "100s of restaurants switched to MyGenie across 75 cities" |
| **Why** | "switched to" implies they came FROM somewhere else — resonates with comparison shoppers |
| **Where to change** | `DemoForm sector="meta-demo"` uses `sector === "meta-demo"` conditional in DemoForm.jsx to render this line (line 380) |
