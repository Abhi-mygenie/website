7. Imagery Guidelines
Real hospitality. Real product. No generic stock.
DO USE
  • Real restaurant/hotel/cafe interiors
  • Product screenshots on phones (owner dashboard, KDS, captain app)
  • Before/After split comparisons
  • Indian hospitality settings
  • Owners looking at their phone (control narrative)
  • Busy kitchen / service scenes
DON'T USE
  • Generic stock laptop photos
  • Western restaurant imagery (not relatable)
  • Abstract tech/AI visuals (circuits, robots)
  • Clip art or cartoon illustrations
  • Emojis as icons
  • Generic "happy team" corporate photos

Signature visual motif: The "Before MyGenie / After MyGenie" split — a recurring, ownable device for ads and landing pages.

8. Ad Copy Tone
Confident, warm, proof-driven — like a successful owner sharing what works

Voice Principles

WRITE LIKE THIS

"Bill in seconds, even at peak rush."

"Catch theft before it eats your profit."

"Owners using MyGenie save up to 25% — here's the proof."

"Running hospitality shouldn't feel like firefighting."

NEVER WRITE THIS

"Experience next-generation transaction velocity."

"Comprehensive audit-logging functionality."

"The most revolutionary, game-changing POS ever built!"

"We deliver enterprise-grade operational excellence solutions."

Power Words

profit margin leakage theft control visibility faster repeat save protect proof from your phone every outlet one app peak rush

Banned Words

revolutionary game-changing next-gen seamless leverage synergy robust empower world-class cutting-edge AI-powered (alone)

4-Beat Ad Formula

1. Pain (name the owner's real problem) → 2. Solution (the MyGenie module) → 3. Outcome (the business result, ideally a number) → 4. CTA (book a demo / see pricing)

"Cashiers cancelling items after payment? MyGenie's audit logs record every void. One owner caught ₹1 lakh in theft in two weeks. See it on your own data — book a demo."
